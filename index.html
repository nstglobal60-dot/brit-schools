About Our School

A place where learning feels like discovery

Britattach Montessori & High School was founded on a simple belief: children learn best when they are respected, guided and given the freedom to explore. From our toddler community through to senior secondary, we build confident, independent and compassionate young people.

  • ✓Authentic Montessori foundation — mixed-age classrooms, prepared environments and self-paced mastery.
  • ✓Strong academic pathway — Science, Arts and Commercial streams with exam-focused support.
  • ✓Character & leadership — clubs, sports, debate, music and community service.
  • ✓Partnership with parents — termly reports, open days and continuous communication.
